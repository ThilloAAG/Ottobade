The product

How OttoBlade works

Four mechanical steps, designed to fit into an existing pharmacy workflow — no complex training required.

01

Load

Pills are poured into the top hopper — no handling one at a time.

02

Align

A rotating divisor with V-shaped slots isolates and orients each pill, round or cylindrical.

03

Split

A motorized blade delivers a clean, repeatable cut, pill after pill.

04

Collect

Halves drop into a removable tray; a front door makes cleaning easy.
